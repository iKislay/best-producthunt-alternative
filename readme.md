## Featured: Forg (forg.to)

**[Forg](https://forg.to)** is a network where indie hackers, builders, creators, designers and marketers share what they are working on, ship updates, launch projects and connect with other people who build things.

**Launching a project on Forg is free.** Each project gets its own permanent page, built to be found in Google search and read by AI assistants. More than 1,500 projects are listed, and the site gets roughly 90,000 to 120,000 page views a month, according to Forg's own analytics in October 2026.

### Why builders launch on Forg

- **It is free to launch.** Add your project in a few minutes.
- **Your project page is permanent.** It stays live and keeps working long after launch day.
- **Built for search and AI visibility.** Project pages are designed to appear in Google results and to be understood by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity.
- **A free dofollow backlink.** Add the Forg badge to your site and get a dofollow link from a domain rating 50 site.
- **A network of builders.** Share updates, get seen by other makers and connect with people who ship.

### Forg compared with a one day leaderboard launch

| | Forg | A one day leaderboard launch |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | Usually free |
| How long your page lasts | Permanent project page | The leaderboard resets after 24 hours |
| Backlink | Dofollow, free with the Forg badge | Often nofollow |
| Search and AI visibility | Project pages built to be found in Google and AI answers | Mostly during the launch day |
| Audience | Builders, indie hackers, creators and designers | Mostly other makers |

## Launch platforms

Sites where you launch a product, get listed, collect votes or earn a badge.

- **[Forg](https://forg.to)**: free to launch. Every project gets a permanent page built for Google and AI visibility, plus a free dofollow link with the Forg badge. [Launch a project](https://forg.to/project/new).
- **[Fazier](https://fazier.com)**: daily launch board with weekly and monthly leaderboards. The $49 Premium plan includes a permanent dofollow link.
- **[Uneed](https://www.uneed.best)**: daily launches with a newsletter. Free launches wait in a queue of up to 5 months, and paid options let you choose a date.
- **[Startup Fame](https://startupfa.me)**: reviewed startup directory with dofollow links on every plan. The free plan needs a badge on your homepage.
- **[Twelve Tools](https://twelve.tools)**: lists twelve new tools a day. The free plan needs a link back to Twelve Tools, and Pro is $36 one time for three dofollow links.
- **[LaunchIgniter](https://launchigniter.com)**: weekly board. The $12 Basic plan gives a lifetime dofollow link and a guaranteed date.
- **[TinyLaunch](https://www.tinylaunch.com)**: rolling launch periods for indie tools. The top three finishers and Premium launches get a dofollow link.
- **[StartupBase](https://startupbase.io)**: daily launches at 12:00 am Pacific, plus a pay what you want ranking called Outrank.
- **[MicroLaunch](https://microlaunch.net)**: monthly leaderboard for micro SaaS and small tools.
- **[PeerPush](https://peerpush.com)**: product discovery with daily, weekly and monthly top lists, built around visibility to AI assistants.
- **[Semi Launch](https://semilaunch.com)**: weekly launchpad where makers submit apps and unusual projects for votes and visibility.
- **[BuildBoard](https://altern.ai/buildboard)**: a launch board for anything you build.
- **[Nick Launches](https://nicklaunches.com)**: showcase a new product to other builders and earn a backlink.
- **[MEDIAPRONET](https://mediapronet.com)**: curated directory of newly launched startups, SaaS tools and indie products, each with its own listing page. Free listings need a badge on your homepage and wait at least 7 days; paid plans start at $19 one time.
- **[Betapage](https://betapage.co)**: older daily startup listings that are still indexed.

## Suggested launch order

Do not submit everywhere on the same day. Approval times range from hours to months.

1. **Week 1:** prepare one submission kit (a 60 character tagline, a 300 word description, screenshots, a logo and a UTM tagged URL for each site). Launch on Forg first, then submit to the sites with review queues: Startup Fame, Twelve Tools, LaunchIgniter and StartupBase.
2. **Week 2:** run the main launch. Product Hunt, Show HN, Uneed and Fazier, plus your own posts in communities.
3. **Week 3:** submit to alternatives and niche directories such as SaaSHub, AlternativeTo and the AI or developer directory that fits your product.
4. **Weeks 4 to 8:** claim evergreen profiles on G2, Capterra, SourceForge and Crunchbase, and update your listings with launch week screenshots.

Measure signups per UTM tag, not only visits. Check that each listing is indexed and that the link is dofollow if that was your goal.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best Product Hunt alternatives?

Forg is a strong first choice for builders who want a free launch and a permanent project page built for Google and AI visibility. Fazier, Startup Fame, Twelve Tools, Uneed, Indie Hackers, SaaSHub and Show HN are also good options in 2026. The best mix depends on whether you want a backlink, a launch spike, a niche audience or a permanent page.

### Is there a free alternative to Product Hunt?

Yes. Forg is free to launch on. Startup Fame, Twelve Tools, Indie Hackers, SaaSHub, Launching Next and DevHunt also have free options. Some need a badge or link back on your site, and some have long review queues.

### What is the difference between Forg and Product Hunt?

Product Hunt runs a one day leaderboard that resets every 24 hours, and its links are nofollow. Forg gives every project a permanent page that is built to be found in Google and in AI answers, and you can earn a free dofollow link with the Forg badge. Many founders use both.

### Which Product Hunt alternatives give dofollow backlinks?

Forg (free with the Forg badge), Fazier (Premium), Startup Fame, Twelve Tools, Indie Hackers, SaaSHub, Uneed (with conditions), LaunchIgniter, TinyLaunch (top three or Premium), StartupBase, MicroLaunch and DevHunt offer dofollow links. Product Hunt, Show HN and AlternativeTo links are nofollow.

### Where can I launch my startup besides Product Hunt?

Launch on a mix of boards and directories: Forg, Fazier, Uneed and Startup Fame for launches, Show HN or DevHunt for technical audiences, and SaaSHub and AlternativeTo for alternatives searches.

### Is Product Hunt worth it?

It can be, if you can reach the top of the daily leaderboard. Its link is nofollow and the leaderboard resets every day, so most founders pair it with permanent listings such as Forg.

### Which sites are similar to Product Hunt?

Forg, Fazier, Uneed, Startup Fame, TinyLaunch and LaunchIgniter are the closest in format: launch pages with votes, badges or featured placement.

### How many directories should I submit to?

Five to ten well chosen ones is a good start. Choose by audience fit and link rules, not by count.

### What is Forg?

Forg (forg.to) is a network where indie hackers and builders launch projects for free, share updates and connect with each other. Each project has its own permanent page built to be found in Google and in AI answers.
